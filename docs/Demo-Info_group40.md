## 8. Recommended Recording Flow

### 8.1 TA Segment

1. Log in as `ta001`.
2. Open the TA Dashboard.
3. Open Profile / Edit Profile and save once.
4. Open My CV and upload a PDF or DOCX file.
5. Open Browse Jobs and search for `J019`.
6. Open the job detail page and show the job information.
7. Click Apply, enter a short statement, and submit.
8. Open My Applications and confirm the new application is visible.

### 8.2 MO Segment

1. Log in as `mo001`.
2. Open Job Management.
3. Open `J020` to show draft status, `J006` to show closed status, and `J004` to show expired status.
4. Click Create New Job and intentionally leave required fields blank to trigger validation.
5. Fill in the required fields and save the job.
6. Open Applicant List and select `J001`.
7. Open `APP001` in the review view.
8. Show the application summary and workload warning state.
9. Click Accept or Reject and show the decision result.

### 8.3 Admin Segment

1. Log in as `admin`.
2. Open the Admin Dashboard.
3. Review the KPI cards and workload table.
4. Explain which applicants are high risk.
5. Click Export and show the workload or application export action.
